I’m Michael Mandahl. I built and led software companies, became a VC, and eventually left tech. I went on to become a licensed psychotherapist and co-found Emotivum, a psychotherapy practice in Stockholm.

Then AI happened. These days, I often find myself up late doing what drew me to software in the first place — imagining what it could become and how it could change the way we work and live.

I’m building **Mira**, an AI tool that maps a conversation as it happens. You can see how ideas connect, what’s been decided, and what’s still open while there’s still time to steer the conversation. Mira is in private development, and I’m aiming to open-source it by the end of 2026.
